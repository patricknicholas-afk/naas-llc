I'm Patrick Nicholas — a product designer and strategist who's spent a decade building digital experiences across fintech, media, healthcare, and consumer tech. The work keeps changing. The obsession with getting it right hasn't.

My Story

I started in objects.

A BFA in Industrial Design from SCAD taught me that good design isn't about the surface; it's about the logic underneath it. The systems and processes of a product lifecycle are fundamental in an evolving physical and digital environment that people are continually connected in.

That instinct for how things are made, and why they hold together, has grounded me through every phase of my career, from interaction design to design systems to the AI-augmented workflows I run today.
